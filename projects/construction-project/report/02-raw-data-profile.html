Section 3Duplicate RFI IDs

No RFI number is repeated. The 112 identifiers are unique, so a later duplicate check does not have to merge conflicting records.

Section 4Dates

107 rows have a received date. All 107 values are Excel serial numbers, not formatted calendar dates. Date conversion belongs in the cleaning step, not in this profile.

Section 7Notes

72 records have notes. Forty notes are blank. The filled notes are the source for a later document-impact check.

Section 8Status

The Status column is blank on all 112 records. The source does not provide an original status to preserve. Any later status, such as Answered or Open, will be a derived field.

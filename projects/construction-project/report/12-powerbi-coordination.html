Section 3Two roles, one party list

The originating party is who asked. The responsible party is who must answer.

In the workbook those are the FROM and TASKED columns. In the star schema they are two keys on the same RFI, both pointing at dim_party: from_party_key for the submitter and tasked_party_key for the party tasked to respond.

On this register the two roles do not share a name. Six contractors submit the questions. Ten government or design parties are asked to answer them.

RoleSource columnWho they are hereWhat the register shows
Originating party FROM The organisation that submitted the RFI DAVID BOLAND, INC submitted 71 of 107. The 8 open RFIs came from Dobco, Inc., Richard Group LCC, and Willdan.
Responsible party TASKED The party assigned to review and respond AE was tasked with 66, and all 66 are answered. CT was tasked with 15, and all 8 open RFIs sit with CT.

Power BI keeps the roles apart. The active relationship is the originating party, so a normal chart of party_name counts submitters. The responsible-party charts turn on the inactive tasked_party_key relationship for that measure only. That is why this page can show both who generated the questions and who still owes the answer.

The received date is on every row. There is no answered date, so this page does not show days open or a response-time target.

Section 5Who is tasked

AE is tasked with 66 of 107 RFIs, about 62%, and all 66 are answered. CT is tasked with 15. Seven of those are answered and eight are open, a response rate of 46.67%. Every other tasked party is at 100%: PM 9, JBMDL 8, AE/JBMDL 4, and one each for AE/JBMDL/PM, AE/PM, CT/PM, JBMDL/AE, and USACE DB.

The open-RFI bar for the tasked party is a single bar: CT, 8. The discipline stack shows what each tasked party was given. Electrical is 38 of AE’s 66, about 58%. All 15 of CT’s RFIs are Other. General is 6 of JBMDL’s 8. Other is 5 of PM’s 9.
